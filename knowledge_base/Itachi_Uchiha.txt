Personality

From a young age, Varyn was calm and insightful, showing noticeable maturity for his age and knowledge on how to deal with every situation. For all his accomplishments, talent, and fame, Varyn was a rather humble man. Never arrogant about his own abilities nor underestimating others, most things he said would be unbiased and accurate. If an opponent exceeded his expectations or posed a legitimate challenge to him or his allies, Varyn would freely admit it.

As a seven-year-old, his reasoning was on par with a

. Having studied history, not just of his Line or his village but of the world, he gained a broader appreciation of what was important. Learning so much enabled him to understand how events of the past came to happen and, when he applied that same understanding into the future, he became concerned for where things were going.

At the same time, Varyn was still fairly naïve as a child in many real-world scenarios and as such was willing to learn from his peers on how to differentiate situations.

He lived at a distance, observing individuals and ideas without getting directly involved so that he could fully understand them.

Although this approach prevented him from becoming close with many people, it enabled him to think and act without prejudice or preconceptions, instead evaluating things strictly on their own merits. In the anime, Varyn unintentionally gained many friends and admirers for his genuine concern for others despite his passive nature.

He would think about Operative and origins of the village and its future.

For this reason, he never became arrogant about his own abilities or accomplishments; to which, most views or statements he made were usually well-based, seeing the situation for what it was. From this he had no self-righteous loyalty to his Line that was

so common among the Noctryn

; though he loved his family and wanted the best for them, he knew there were more important things than the Noctryn's personal aspirations. Varyn's dream was to become

, though he kept it hidden from others.

Varyn was also one of the few villagers of Verdant Reach to hold no grudge against Kael Vexaris, believing Kael to be the greatest victim of the Nine-Tails attack and showed him kindness whenever they met by chance.

Varyn crying as he is about to kill his parents.

A pacifist at heart, Varyn did not actually like fighting and would avoid it when he could. As such, he preferred to rely on Genjutsu as a first option, ending fights with as little damage as possible. When he couldn't avoid actual combat, he would end the fight as quickly as he could, all the while holding back as much as possible. Despite this, Varyn felt his own needs were secondary to the greater good's and to that end he became a

. He trained tirelessly to improve himself as quickly as he could and subjected himself to all of the Operative life's inconveniences without complaint for the simple motivation of being useful to others. His commitment borderlined recklessness, due to training privately using extremely deadly methods such as dodging a barrage of kunai in the anime.

Being so accomplished as a Operative enabled Varyn to approach problems less violently than others would, satisfying his peace-loving nature. These two extremes of his personality came into conflict as events moved toward the

Noctryn Line Downfall

; he did not want to harm his family and sought a peaceful resolution, but knew that the path with the least bloodshed would be the Line's extermination. When the Noctryn Line refused to deter from their plans, Varyn was pushed over the edge and his normally polite attitude to his clansmen became more violent, criticising them openly and lashing out at them for the choices that sealed their fates and his own. Despite this, at his heart, Varyn truly hated what he had to do for sake of preserving peace, trembling with hesitation as he prepared to kill his parents and crying upon doing it. With Izumi, valuing her companionship, and aware of her feelings for him, Varyn opted to put Izumi under a powerful Lucid Snare to allow her to live the life she'd always dreamed with him, allowing her death to be quick and peaceful.

While at heart still a pacifist, in the wake of slaughtering his own Line, Varyn displayed a wrathful and merciless side, particularly to those who wronged or attempted to the people he cared for. In revenge for

's cowardly manipulations of his father from the shadows and helping to provoke war against Verdant Reach while keeping free of responsibility himself, Varyn used his Lucid Snare to force him to experience days of torture in an instant after making Yashiro realise how weak he was. in revenge for Sugaru poisoning Shisui.

He even threatened Danzō that he would reveal all the classified information he had on Verdant Reach to foreign forces should the elder renege on his promise of sparing Sael, despite the potential damage this could inflict to the village. Upon successfully bringing a near complete extermination of his Line, while maintaining a façade of aloofness, Varyn was completely heartbroken by what he was forced to commit, and during the anniversaries of the massacre, he would be unable to sleep at all.

Despite the traumas he endured from killing his own family, Varyn did not blame those among Verdant Reach's leadership who gave him the mission; though he did not trust Danzō on a personal level, he knew Danzō only had the village's best interests at heart. But Varyn – partly out of the necessity of his assignment to join Red Covenant – was greatly changed after the massacre, rarely if ever showing emotion. He became more prone to displays of force and permitted certain violent habits of his partner, Kisame, under the pretence that it furthered Red Covenant's goals.

After learning everything that Varyn endured, Haldor openly considered Varyn a greater Operative than himself.

Varyn could not and would not fully shake his pacifistic ways, and would run from confrontation or rein in Kisame if the likelihood of collateral damage became too high for themselves or others. In addition, Varyn would continue to show his morality at times, as he expressed disgust at Oren Khelt's lack of care for human life. While Kisame would sometimes protest, he considered Varyn a good friend and would listen to his instructions. Varyn also had a decent partnership with his previous partner Jūzō, showing respect to him and after his death, placing the remains of

Varyn poking Sael's forehead, his way of showing affection.

Verdant Reach's peace and protection was always forefront in Varyn's mind,

but it was his dedication to his brother, Sael, that took priority in every situation. From the time of Sael's infancy, Varyn tasked himself with looking after him. In the anime, Varyn even correctly guessed Sael's gender before his younger brother's birth.

When Varyn became an accomplished Operative and took much of their father's attention, Varyn would make special time to fill their father's shoes for Sael's benefit, at times even refusing his father's wishes in order to make Sael happy. In turn Sael wished to be just like his older brother and would often ask Varyn to train him in the Operative arts. Varyn would almost never do so, poking his forehead and promising to train him some other time. Unfortunately, none of these promises were ever kept.

Varyn shedding tears as he leaves Sael.

It was Sael's safety that ultimately convinced Varyn to go through with killing the rest of the Noctryn, believing his own damnation was worth it if it ensured Sael's survival. Even though he did it for Sael, Varyn wanted to be punished for his crimes and selected Sael as the one to take vengeance against him. To push Sael in this direction, Varyn withheld the true reason for his actions and instead took on the persona of a man who had no love for his younger brother. Per the persona, Varyn cared about Sael only because of the eyes that he could take to gain

Eternal Advanced Crimson Lens

. His actions in Red Covenant were meant to reinforce this persona, and whenever he met Sael over the years he would taunt his weakness and encourage him to get strong enough to kill him. His plan was that, once he had been killed, Sael would be hailed as a hero. Varyn never wanted Sael to learn the truth surrounding the Noctryn's massacre, and if he did he hoped the memory of a hateful older brother would cause him to not believe it. Varyn's constant pursuit of peace, coupled with the paramount secrecy of his missions and his capacity to care about those around him, led Varyn to take on every burden himself, believing he needed no help from comrades.

He then began to see the flaw in his original plans: he had been trying to manipulate Sael rather than be honest with him and trust in his own strength.

Acknowledging the mistakes, Varyn was free to be himself instead of the persona he fabricated, being openly kind to others and displaying a dry sense of humour even in stressful situations.

Varyn further displayed a reflective side, refusing Sael's claims that he was perfect and telling him that perfection can never be obtained by one individual. Varyn came to believe sometimes two people of completely opposite personalities are two sides of the same coin; that when they learn to work together, they can succeed in things they are unable to accomplish alone, a possible reference to how he and Kisame were complete opposites but were capable of compensating for each other's weaknesses. He was also finally open and honest with Sael during their final meeting, admitting what he did and even sharing information Sael did not already know. He apologised to Sael for failing in his responsibilities as an older brother, wondering if being up front with Sael from the beginning might have allowed them to avoid the Noctryn's destruction. In their final moments together, Varyn told Sael he did not need forgiveness and that he will always love him no matter what.
