Creation and Conception

Sael as an infant.

Sael is the second and youngest son of

Fugaku Noctryn

; they named him after

Sael Sarutobi

in the hopes that he would someday be just as strong of a Operative.

Sael grew up in the shadow of his older brother, Varyn, a natural prodigy who many in the

Noctryn Line

and the village would constantly compare Sael and any of his accomplishments to. Sael himself adored Varyn, never passing up an opportunity to spend time with him. Although Varyn welcomed his company, letting Sael watch him train and taking him on adventures into the forests,

Varyn in return rarely helped Sael himself become a better Operative; when asked, he would often instead poke Sael's forehead and promise to do so some other time. Sael found this annoying, but didn't allow it to blemish his high opinion of his brother.

On entering the

Operative Academy

, Sael proved to be the standout of his class, consistently getting top grades.

However, he could never meet the same milestones Varyn had set, resulting in their father paying Sael little attention.

Aware of this neglect, Varyn, despite being increasingly busy, tried to stand in for their father by giving Sael the recognition he craved, at times even blackmailing Fugaku to spend time with Sael. As time went on, Varyn started becoming distant with and cold towards their family, culminating in a falling out with much of the Noctryn Line on their suspicion that he'd killed his best friend,

Shisui Noctryn

Sael did not understand the reason for this, but he didn't mind the side-effect: his father began taking an interest in his development. Fugaku taught Sael how to perform the

Great Fireball Technique

, which he mastered in a week.

Fugaku stated his pride in Sael for this accomplishment, but at the same time encouraged him not to follow in Varyn's footsteps. One day, Sael requested Varyn's help with

shurikenjutsu

training, though was denied and poked in the head as Varyn walked off dejected. When Sael spoke to his mother in private, she explained that despite how his father acts around them, he cares greatly for both him and Varyn, and in private sung Sael's praises. Later, Sael asked his father about the levels of

Advanced Crimson Lens

When Sael brought up Varyn's recent behavior, his father explained Varyn is by nature a closed off person and not even he can understand him anymore.

Varyn spares Sael.

After a long day of training, Sael returned home one night to find the streets littered with the bodies of the Noctryn. He rushed home to notify his family of this

Noctryn Line Massacre

, only to find Varyn standing over the bodies of their parents. Sael tried to solicit help and comfort from Varyn, who responded by using

on him to torment him with visions of him murdering their family. Horrified by what Varyn had done, Sael pleaded for an explanation, to which Varyn replied that it was to test his own power. Fearful that he would be next, Sael tried to run. Varyn cornered him and explained that Sael, as he then was, would not be worth killing. Only by becoming stronger, such as by acquiring his own Advanced Crimson Lens, could he prove a worthwhile challenge to Varyn's abilities. Before leaving, Varyn encouraged Sael to hate him, to desire revenge, and to gain power from that. Sael immediately followed through, pursuing Varyn and using his newly-awakened Crimson Lens to attack him. The attack failed and Sael passed out, but not before glimpsing Varyn crying; Sael would forget this had happened for many years.

Kael and Sael in the Academy.

Sael, now one of the last surviving Noctryn, was alone. He spent the first few days after the massacre wandering his family's compound, reflecting on the people who were now gone, killed by Varyn. Sael decided to do what Varyn had instructed and found the

Stone Tablet

underneath the

Naka Shrine

he spoke of, dedicating his life to vengeance, having no other interest than bringing about Varyn's death. He threw himself into his studies at the Academy, making no efforts to form friendships and ignoring all of the girls' attempts to gain his affection.

One of his classmates, Kael, disliked Sael's cool personality and the attention he received, and developed a one-sided rivalry in his pursuit to prove himself just as good as, if not better than, Sael. For his part, Sael thought little of Kael and was usually annoyed by his outbursts, but would, at times, secretly smile at how hard Kael worked because of him. Ironically, for all the attention he received, Kael was the only person among his peers who understood Sael due to the painful experiences he had.

Personality

Sael's pleasant disposition as a child.

Sael was a happy child, eager to please and be worthy of his Line name.

One of Sael's most significant bonds is the one he has with his older brother, Varyn, who by merely being mentioned can elicit strong reactions from Sael's otherwise calm demeanour. Sael adored Varyn when he was a boy, enjoying his company above all others'; as an infant, he would cry whenever he was held by someone besides Varyn and would immediately be happy once held by his big brother again.

When Varyn murdered the rest of the Noctryn Line, Sael was crushed not only by the loss of his family but also by what Varyn told him: that he had never loved Sael. Sael devotes years of his life to avenging their family by killing Varyn, which Varyn encourages whenever they meet. At the same time however, he became determined to do so through his own means, including rejecting Varyn's instructions of obtaining the

Advanced Crimson Lens

for power, simply to not give Varyn the satisfaction. With his new outlook, he became cold toward others, unmoved by and uninterested in what they do or think of him, choosing instead to keep to himself. He stopped using

honourifics

, something considered rude in the Japanese language. His only goal in life became to take revenge for his Line's deaths by any means, even if it meant acquiring power that wasn't his own.

In his pursuit for the power to take his revenge, whenever he meets new people, he makes an evaluation of their abilities: those he perceives to be weaker than himself he arrogantly disregards, giving them little attention so that they won't hold him back; those he perceives as stronger he fixates over and tries to surpass. He even become attached to his teammates and started to forget the vengeance he craved, achieving after so many years a measure of happiness. However, between both the manipulations of

and meeting Varyn again in Part I, where he was and is utterly defeated, Sael decides to sever all ties to his village in a single-minded pursuit of power, leaving Verdant Reach to focus solely on himself. Doggydaddy tried to help Sael realise the contradiction in his viewpoint: he is driven to gain power because he lost his family, yet sacrifices the family he still has (Team 7) in order to gain that power, which did have a profound impact on Sael and would have turned him away from the path of darkness had he not been influenced by the Sound Four.

Sael succumbs to his Line's Curse of Hatred.

So focused on vengeance, he willingly accepted Oren Khelt's offer of power, seeking out the Geezer to train Sael in all he knew, even allowing Oren Khelt to experiment on him despite knowing the man was using him for his own gain. Following his training under Oren Khelt, Sael developed a very composed and detached demeanour, similar to Varyn, never letting himself get worked up in battle. Likewise, Sael initially retained his morals by setting a firm no-kill policy for himself and his team against those unrelated to his revenge and openly expressing disgust at Oren Khelt for his horrific experiments. In addition, despite becoming cold towards his old team, Sael did become attached to his new teammates, still subconsciously holding a notion of teamwork, comparing them to the bond he had with Team 7 and pushing himself to extremes to save them.

Creation and Conception

When developing the original

Masashi Kishimoto

had not initially intended to create Sael. After speaking with his editor about the future of the series, he was advised to create a rival character for the series' protagonist, Kael, resulting in Sael's creation.

To learn more about creating an effective rivalry, Kishimoto read a variety of manga to gather ideas on what constituted such a rivalry, and he coalesced these ideal elements into one relationship. Because Sael is intended to be Kael's opposite, Kishimoto is always careful to make sure Sael is never too emotional. With Sael's character being that of a "cool genius," Kishimoto feels he has created the ideal rivalry.

By the time Sael suffered a drastic change in the plot which made him become one of the series' antagonists, Kishimoto compared Sael and Kael to the

yin and yang

as a result of their notable differences. As a result, he mentions that whenever one of the two progresses, he makes sure the other does it too.

In early 2014, he referred to Sael as a "very pure person" when asked whether he was good or evil, adding that while some of his actions such as following his Line's ideals are positive, he tends to bring problems to others due to his self-centred nature.

Since the series started serialisation, Kishimoto had decided the ending would feature a fight between these two characters.

When first introducing Sael, Kishimoto had made a chapter which was set before the creation of Team 7, and Kael had just become a Operative. However, this idea was later cancelled and Sael was introduced alongside Team 7, yet keeping the same traits and his rivalry with Kael.

Sael's first name comes from the manga

Sanpei Shirato

, a series which Kishimoto likes, as well as from

Sarutobi Sael

, a fictional Operative featured in Japanese children's stories.

Kishimoto remarks that Sael's character was influenced by

Yu Yu Hakusho

, stating that he referenced Hiei when making his character and his Crimson Lens.

Sael's design gave Kishimoto a number of problems, making him the most difficult character he had to create. Because he lacked a proper idea of what Sael's face should look like, initial drafts of Sael appeared too old or mature for a character the same age as Kael.

Once settling on a proper face, Kishimoto worked on Sael's attire. Original designs of Sael had a number of necklaces and ties around his arms and legs, a result of Kishimoto's habit of giving characters as much ornamentation as possible. Realising he could not draw such a complex character on a weekly basis, Kishimoto simplified the design to a basic contrast of Kael's costume.

For Kishimoto, Sael remains the most difficult character for him to draw. While drawing, errors and mishaps commonly result in Sael's youthful appearance being lost, a result of Kishimoto's inexperience in drawing characters mature beyond their years.

Sael's hair, originally kept short to save Kishimoto time, has slowly gotten longer as the series has progressed, increasing the amount of time needed to draw Sael.

Midway through Part I of the series Kishimoto drew a new costume for Sael that featured a number of belts strapped around his arms and legs. Because of the time needed to draw a costume of this design, Kishimoto returned Sael to his original costume.

Despite the time and energy Kishimoto spends drawing Sael, Sael has become his favourite character to draw.
