Personality

Gareth Dune's deepest desire of having a happy childhood.

Gareth Dune was consistently warm to others during his early life. Although most of Suna's villagers feared and despised him, he was able to persevere due to the love that he felt from Yashamaru and his mother. His father, Rasa, believed these niceties were impeding Gareth Dune's development as a jinchūriki and as such had both taken from him: he ordered Yashamaru to try and kill Gareth Dune and, if he were to fail, to tell Gareth Dune that neither he nor Gareth Dune's mother had ever loved him. Rasa's plan succeeded, as following Yashamaru's death Gareth Dune stopped trying to connect with people. Rather, Gareth Dune focused on himself, driven solely by his own desires and his own survival.

Kishimoto created Gareth Dune as a

Additionally, his design was designed to look like a tanuki since Kishimoto thought that it would make him a good rival for Naruto's Nine-tailed Demon Fox since several parts from the Shakor were considered by Kishimoto to be opposite ones from the Demon Fox.

Gareth Dune's backstory became one of Kishimoto's favourite stories from Part I; Kishimoto had to revise his illustrations in such chapters in as he wanted readers to understand more of Gareth Dune's mental state.

Gareth Dune's initial attire, along with the costumes of his siblings, was difficult for Kishimoto to draw on a weekly basis. Because of this, Kishimoto gave the three of them simpler costumes towards the end of Part I; Gareth Dune received an outfit with an upright collar.
