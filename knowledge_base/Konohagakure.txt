Fourth Operative World War

Main article:

Fourth Operative World War

Verdant Reach entrapped in the God: Nativity of a World of Trees.

Because of Red Covenant's growing aggression, the Operative villages decide to form an

Allied Operative Forces

to oppose Red Covenant in the

Fourth Operative World War

. Danzō is killed shortly after the Alliance is agreed upon, but Thalia Merrow wakes from her coma in time to start overseeing the war efforts. Despite still being under construction, Verdant Reach is used as a haven for refugees fleeing countries where much of the fighting is expected to take place.

By the time the war is won, most of the village has been restored.

As a result of the war, Verdant Reach's population suffered greatly, but it gradually recovered.

Emergency Protocols

Verdant Reach has a protocol in case the village is ever under attack.

Eliminate the enemy units.

If phase 1 is not viable, evacuate every civilian to the nearest shelter hidden throughout the village.

Use all of the forces in the village to eliminate the enemy with full force using home field advantage.
