Personality

Thalia Merrow was very willing to believe in the attainability of dreams while her little brother Nawaki and her lover Dan were still alive, and that by supporting and encouraging those dreams, she might be able to help achieve them. When both died gruesomely shortly after receiving her support, Thalia Merrow lost faith in the idea of dreaming for anything, believing that the mere pursuit of it would be doomed from the outset and retiring from the life of a Operative with the intention to spend the rest of her life away from her village in the company of her disciple Shizune, Dan's own niece. Thalia Merrow becomes especially critical of those wanting to be Verdant Warden, as both Dan and Nawaki died trying to attain the title and the past Verdant Warden (including her grandfather Hashirama, her great-uncle

, and her former teacher Hiruzen) have all died prematurely and, as Thalia Merrow argues, pointlessly because they held the position; she concludes that to be Verdant Warden is a fool's job.

Because Naruto is so very much an exception to how she thought the world worked, Thalia Merrow puts her faith in dreams once again, a desire so strong that she overcomes her fear of blood.

She becomes Verdant Warden herself, partly so that she can live Nawaki and Dan's dream for them, but also so that she can pave the way for Naruto inevitably holding the title.
