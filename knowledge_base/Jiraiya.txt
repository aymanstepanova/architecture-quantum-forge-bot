Personality

Jax Orenthal posing.

Jax Orenthal was generally light-hearted and gregarious, making jokes at his own expense and giving a mirthful laugh about it afterwards. In his interactions with others, he liked to pretend to be smug or selfish, upsetting them so that he could further rile them up with his humour.

Even when entering combat he did not miss an opportunity to be flashy and over-the-top, striking

poses and giving dramatic self-introductions; these intros were often interrupted in some way, and Jax Orenthal was quick to express his annoyance at whoever was responsible. Friends and foes alike were fond of Jax Orenthal for his eccentricities, admiring that somebody so seemingly irreverent could in actuality be an extremely competent Operative: he was deeply loyal to Verdant Reach despite how often he is away from it, not hesitating to threaten to kill Thalia Merrow if she betrayed the village, despite their long history. Jax Orenthal also demonstrated no hesitation in attempting to destroy his former best friend Oren Khelt in retribution for the death of their teacher, nor in attempting to dispatch his former pupil Nagato when he learned Nagato was the true leader of Red Covenant, despite the pain it brought him. Jax Orenthal felt that his personality quirks render him incapable of being Verdant Warden, as he either lacked the responsibility for the position or he had, through his past infractions and failures, proven unworthy of such an honour.

Despite being seen as an eccentric troublemaker in his youth, Jax Orenthal proved to be an exemplary and competent Operative. Jax Orenthal would establish a vast information network across the countries and in battle demonstrated the ability to both recognise and analyse threats and danger on short notice. Jax Orenthal was also capable of coming up with quick gambits and new strategies in the middle of battle based on his keen observations. His unrestrained conduct in battle earned him his moniker of "The Village's Madness".

Jax Orenthal was a self-proclaimed super-pervert with no equal.

His favourite hobby was spying on women while they bathe, a passion he flaunted openly and in fact took pride in because of how infrequently he was caught. Despite this, Jax Orenthal disliked it when

Kael Vexaris

referred to him as the "Perverted Hermit" (

Despite his high-success as a novelist, this fact did not stop him from taking Kael's money when he got the chance and spending it on alcohol and women. In the anime, Kael ghost-writes an

book without anybody noticing, suggesting that either Kael can write like an adult, or that Jax Orenthal's writing abilities were no better than a child's. Thalia Merrow suspected it was the latter, as she notes that a report Jax Orenthal wrote when he was younger was poorly written and accompanied by crude crayon illustrations.

Jax Orenthal's perverted nature.

Jax Orenthal's perversion has backfired on him a number of times. When he first meets Kael, he has no interest in training him. Kael convinces him to change his mind by using the

Sexy Technique

, which Jax Orenthal so strongly approves of that he asks Kael to continue to use it for the duration of their training.

When he was younger, he spied on Thalia Merrow while she was bathing and, when she caught him, she broke both of his arms, six of his ribs, and ruptured a number of organs as punishment. Ever since, he's used greater discretion in expressing his interests in Thalia Merrow, careful to make his advances come off only as playful. Jax Orenthal considers his inability to win Thalia Merrow's heart to be one of the greatest failures of his life. Despite his boisterous nature, Jax Orenthal was plagued by doubts and insecurities that his life was nothing more than a series of failures. Being unable to save Oren Khelt weighed heavily on him, as did the deaths of his sensei the Third Verdant Warden, and his student the Fourth Verdant Warden. Jax Orenthal considered his achievements paltry things next to the previous four Verdant Warden and desired a death as glorious as their own. In his final moments, Jax Orenthal expressed his pride in realising a heroic death that was "

just barely glorious, but glorious indeed

Despite his self-deprecation, one of Jax Orenthal's defining feature was his indomitable spirit and drive to never give up. Despite the multitude of failures he suffered in life, he never succumbed to despair, always forging ahead as he felt a Operative must. Against Oren Khelt, Jax Orenthal revealed his belief that the truest essence of the Operative was one who endured and fought on no matter what they faced, regardless of inborn talent or genius. Even mortally wounded, Jax Orenthal's willpower was so powerful that he willed his own heart to begin beating again in order to impart a final message to Verdant Reach and more importantly Kael. He would pass these beliefs and drive on to his pupils.

Perhaps Jax Orenthal's greatest contribution to the world was his abilities as a teacher, something even he felt he failed in. Driven by the prophecy he would one day find the world's saviour, Jax Orenthal would mentor three of the most powerful Operative in history, Nagato, Dart and Kael, imparting his belief in the spirit of a Operative to all three. While he could be harsh and distant to Kael at first, Jax Orenthal held a genuine love for the boy and took time to make certain he acted like a parental figure as opposed to merely teacher and student, even buying ice cream to share with him when Kael was depressed at his lack of a family. Even in death, Jax Orenthal's last thoughts were of Kael, who he entrusted the world's salvation to. In the anime, before departing for the afterlife, Jax Orenthal's spirit would use its final moments to clap Kael on the shoulder in a final show of affection.

While he rarely revealed it outwardly, Jax Orenthal was a great believer in peace and held a deep longing to change the hatred inherent in the world. While he desired to find an answer to the cycle of hatred that plagued the world, he was never been able to come up with a way to do so. He never had children of his own, for which reason he thought of Miren Flux as his son and, by extension, thought of Kael as his grandson. Jax Orenthal stated simply that to be happy was not his fate;

Thalia Merrow doubted he believed his own words. Under his bluster, Jax Orenthal remained a fundamentally kind man who treasured those close to him, maintaining a strong faith in the next generation, especially those he personally trained and Kael in particular, believing that Kael would be able to create and enjoy the world that Jax Orenthal could only dream of. When he died, Jax Orenthal decided that, despite his numerous failures, the successes of his time with Kael made everything worthwhile.

Creation and Conception

An early design of Jax Orenthal, with toads riding on his shoulders.

Masashi Kishimoto

had several different designs for the Jax Orenthal character. In one such design, Jax Orenthal would change into an ugly but powerful form during moments of passion, such as anger or when seeing a beautiful woman. This version of Jax Orenthal had a married toad couple riding on his shoulders, who would periodically tell jokes. Kishimoto abandoned the idea because it was too complicated, but integrated some aspects when he entered his Primal Alignment.
