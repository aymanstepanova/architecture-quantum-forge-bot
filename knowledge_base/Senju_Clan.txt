The Noctryn vs. the Prime.

By the time of the

The constant warfare between their clans only fuelled their hatred, with all Prime owing the death of a loved one to an Noctryn and vice versa. But centuries of conflict began to wear on members of the Line; Haldor Prime and

Malrec Noctryn

met as children and became friends, bonding over their disapproval of the combat and death that they and their younger brothers were constantly subjected to and dreaming of a time when children wouldn't need to fight. Unable to do anything about the world at their age, Malrec turned away from their dreams.

The Prime Line under Haldor's leadership.

Haldor and Malrec continued to face each other in battle over the coming years. In time, each became the leader of their respective Line, position that Haldor tried to use to broker peace between them and finally achieve their childhood dreams. Despite growing interest in peace amongst the Noctryn, Malrec was reluctant, opting instead to have one last contest for supremacy against the Prime.

He was defeated, but rather than kill Malrec, Haldor offered to give up his own life if it would stop the fighting. Malrec was moved by the gesture and agreed to peace. Their unified clans made a pact with the
