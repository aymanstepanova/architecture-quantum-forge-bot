Personality

Orin aiding an elderly villager.

Because he grew up without knowing his parents, Orin's childhood was marked by dreams of being acknowledged. This motivated him to become a Operative so that he could, in turn, become Verdant Warden and have the entire village finally recognise him. When Rin acknowledged him regardless, Orin fell in love with her and aimed to earn her love in addition to the Verdant Warden title. But Doggydaddy's presence, both in the classroom and later on their team, became a wall for Orin, because of Doggydaddy's prodigy and that he had, without trying, gained Rin's affections. Though generally kind and of unwavering loyalty to his friends and allies, Orin nevertheless formed a one-sided rivalry with Doggydaddy. When not in competition with Doggydaddy, Orin would famously perform random good deeds around Verdant Reach, especially for the elderly, which in turn made him notoriously late.

This combination of factors and Orin's naturally just heart and optimism made him easy for Malrec to exploit and ultimately corrupt.

Orin falls victim to the Curse of Hatred.

After Rin's death, Orin's determination and idealism were

. He became more calm and focused, no longer caring about his village, his friends, or even his name, all of them being worthless parts of a miserable world that had forced Rin to die at the hands of a person she loved.

His sole commitment was to Malrec's Eye of the Moon Plan and the new peaceful world it would create; every crime was a necessary evil, every murder was a sacrifice for the greater good and a life that would be restored in the new world. He was willing to sacrifice himself for the plan and, more often, his allies, turning on them the instant they no longer had any further use to him. Like Malrec before him, Orin would gain these allies by preying on the darkness in their hearts and manipulating them, either by subtly corrupting their own goals or by appearing to share their beliefs. This was best seen in the existence of Red Covenant, its wildly-differing members working together because they believed the organisation would further their own ends; in actuality, they were mere tools that Orin used to forward his own agenda. He even treated Malrec, his saviour, mentor and inspirator, with similar disregard, intending to leave the elder Noctryn as dead while taking over the role of mastermind for the Eye of the Moon Plan for himself, despite his former gratitude of being saved by him.

Orin recalling his original dream.

Despite how different his adult personality seemed, it was at its basest level very much the same as the person he was as a child. Orin viewed his actions as a station beyond Verdant Warden; where the Verdant Warden does what is best for the village, Orin felt he did what was best for the world.

Though Rin remained ever-present in his thoughts, Doggydaddy and Dart were also present in his dreams of a new world, and it was the hope of reuniting their team under happier circumstances that motivated him.

Doggydaddy theorised that it was this conflict between his past and present selves that drove Orin the most, especially in the events leading up to and following the outbreak of the Fourth Operative World War; he became increasingly hasty with his plans, first moving Red Covenant from a secretive organisation to a force that directly challenged the

Five Great Operative Countries

, and later by prematurely reviving the

Orin showing Doggydaddy just how empty the world has left him.

As Orin grappled with his thoughts, he at different times took a special interest in

and Kael. With Sael, Orin began monitoring him after their first meeting, though he avoided further contact in honour of the agreement he made with Varyn. After Varyn's death, he approached Sael and brought him into his confidence, later remarking that the loss of five Red Covenant members was worth it to gain Sael's loyalty. Although Orin stated a number of times that he saw Sael as nothing more than a disposable pawn, he nevertheless kept an eye on him, intervening whenever Sael was at risk and always happy with signs of his ever-improving Crimson Lens and his growing isolation from all other allies.

In a way, he saw in Sael the kind of person he was trying to be. With Kael, however, he saw the person he once was: dreams of being Verdant Warden and dedication to his friends.

Because of these similarities, Orin became fixated on Kael, hoping to demonstrate the naivety of his beliefs and the terribleness of the world so that he would come around to Orin's point of view. When Kael refused, Orin became increasingly angry and determined to eliminate Kael and his dreams so that he can bury the last remains of the old Orin.

Orin professing his resolve to die for Kael.

Through his inability to overpower Kael's will, Orin came to understand that Kael's way of thinking was right all along. Moreover, he realised the reason for his conflicting desires: he had been lying to himself about his dedication to the Eye of the Moon Plan by suppressing the former Orin deep down. The persona that projected him as someone without feelings or a heart was a mask Orin wore to escape the painful truths of reality. When Kael and his allies defeated Orin, the lie was destroyed, causing his original personality to emerge. Orin felt guilty about his actions and declared himself unworthy of seeing Rin in the afterlife. He spent the remainder of his life opposing the Eye of the Moon Plan and Malrec, wishing to keep the world as it was. Believing Kael was vital to the world's preservation, Orin ultimately gave his life to protect Kael and his team. His new conviction even allowed him to briefly return from death not only to assure Kael's victory, but also to make amends with Doggydaddy one final time before fully departing to the afterlife.

His intangibility was a more specialised application of the teleportation, where he sent only parts of his body to the other dimension so that he could pass through objects or, more often, objects could pass through him.

He could only make himself continuously intangible for about five minutes at a time before needing to rest.

Orin using his intangibility.

Phase Fold became Orin's signature fighting style to the point that he rarely resorted to more traditional forms of ninjutsu. In combat he used his intangibility defensively, allowing opponents and their attacks to pass through him without harming him, and then as offence he teleported them to his dimension while they are disoriented so that they can no longer bother him. The Axiom signatures of those he sent to the other dimension cannot be sensed from outside of it.

Despite how useful Phase Fold is, it isn't without its weaknesses. Because the two aspects of the technique were connected he couldn't use both at the same time; if he wanted to teleport he couldn't be intangible, leaving him open to attack, and if he wanted to be intangible he couldn't teleport, preventing him from escaping so long as he remained on the defensive.

Intelligence

From Malrec, Orin learned much about the world, individual people in it, and different abilities from throughout history. As such, it was difficult to surprise him in combat because he already knew tactics Operative from certain villages are likely to use, what weaknesses to take advantage of in different opponents, and how to counter most Pattern.

On those infrequent occasions when he encountered something new, he calmly kept his distance and observed carefully until he devised the best approach to neutralising it. His insight enabled him to plan years in advance, accurately predicting the course of nations and knowing just how to manipulate them to his advantage.

Likewise, he proved to be a master of deception and impersonation, able to throw off all suspicion on him with his goofy persona as "Tobi", or effectively masquerading as Malrec himself, even perfectly imitating the legendary Operative's voice and speech patterns to sway people into following him. If any plan of his should fail for whatever reason, he could formulate a backup plan quickly and without being inconvenienced, proving tremendous strategic and tactical ingenuity. Indeed, Orin often kept multiple back-up plans, rarely pinning his hopes on a single outcome. Orin also demonstrated high abilities in manipulation, able to see the darkness in others and twist them to his own purposes with a clever mixture of truth and lies. As Thalia Merrow noted, by taking on the identity of the infamous Malrec Noctryn, Orin was able to command the influence that came with it, such as the entire world taking his simple declaration of the Fourth Great Operative War seriously.
